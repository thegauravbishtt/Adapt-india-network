Leased line provider in Okhla

Okhla is one of Delhi's busiest industrial and office districts. Garment export houses, printing presses, pharma distributors, e-commerce warehouses and corporate offices sit side by side across Phase 1, 2 and 3. Many of these businesses send large design files to buyers abroad, run ERP across several units and keep CCTV on every floor, which is more than shared broadband can carry reliably.

We provide dedicated Internet Leased Lines, SD-WAN and MPLS in Okhla through Tata Tele Business Services and ACT Fibernet Enterprise, with one team handling the survey, order, installation and support.

Getting a line into a Okhla building

Mixed buildings

Okhla has old factory buildings, converted units and newer office blocks in the same lane. Newer blocks are usually quicker to connect; older ones may need a fresh fibre run, so we check the building first.

Several units, one business

Many exporters and manufacturers have two or three units in Okhla plus an office elsewhere. Point-to-point links or SD-WAN can join them into one network instead of separate connections.

Upload matters

Sending tech packs, artwork and photos to overseas buyers is upload-heavy. A leased line gives the same speed up as down, which is where broadband usually falls short.

Businesses we connect in Okhla

Garment and textile exporters

Export houses exchange large files and hold video calls with buyers in other time zones. A dedicated line keeps those calls clear in the evening, when broadband slows down.

Printing and packaging

Print units receive heavy artwork files and run machines linked to office software. Steady bandwidth and a static IP for remote access help keep jobs moving.

Warehouses and distributors

Pharma distributors and e-commerce warehouses depend on WMS, scanners and dispatch portals. A main line plus a backup on a different network keeps dispatch going.

Corporate offices

Offices in the newer Okhla blocks need the same as any corporate floor: fast internet, Wi-Fi for everyone and secure links to branches.

Leased line questions from Okhla

Do you provide leased lines in Okhla Phase 1, 2 and 3?

+

Yes. We cover all three phases of Okhla Industrial Area, Okhla Industrial Estate and nearby areas like Jasola and Mohan Cooperative. We check carrier availability at your exact building before quoting.

Can you connect two of our units in Okhla to each other?

+

Yes. Units can be joined with a point-to-point leased line for a private link, or with SD-WAN if you also want to bring in an office or warehouse in another part of Delhi NCR.

Our factory building is old. Can it still get a leased line?

+

Usually, yes. Older buildings sometimes need a new fibre run from the road and permission from the owner. We find that out at the survey so you know what is needed before you commit.

Do you provide a static IP for remote access and CCTV?

+

Yes. Leased lines come with static public IP addresses, which you can use for remote CCTV viewing, VPN access for staff and connecting to buyer or bank portals that need a fixed IP.
