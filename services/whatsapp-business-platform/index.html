WhatsApp Business Platform, in plain words

The WhatsApp Business Platform lets businesses send updates and handle customer conversations on WhatsApp from a business number, connected to their systems rather than a single phone.

Teams can share one number, messages can be automated from your CRM or order system, and customers get updates on the app they already use every day.

Questions about WhatsApp Business Platform

Is this different from the WhatsApp Business app?

+

Yes. The app is for one phone and small volumes. The platform (API) is for businesses that need multiple agents, automation and integration.

Can we send promotional messages?

+

Yes, to customers who have opted in, using approved templates and following WhatsApp's policies.

How are WhatsApp messages charged?

+

Meta charges per conversation, with rates depending on the category (utility, marketing, authentication or service). We'll explain the current pricing.

Can we get the green verified tick?

+

Verification badges are granted by Meta based on its criteria. We can help you apply when eligible.

Is WhatsApp Business Platform available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
