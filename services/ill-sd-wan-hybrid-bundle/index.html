Why businesses choose ILL + SD-WAN Hybrid Bundle

Not every site needs the same connection. A hybrid design puts leased lines where reliability matters most, such as head office and plants, and lower-cost broadband at smaller branches.

SD-WAN joins them into one secure network with automatic failover, and the whole thing comes on one contract with one support desk.

What's included

  • Right link per siteLeased line or broadband depending on importance.
  • Managed SD-WANOne network across all locations.
  • Backup everywhereSecondary links with automatic switching.
  • One contractSingle bill and support desk.

Best suited to

  • Companies with 10+ sites
  • Businesses replacing MPLS
  • Manufacturers with plants and offices
  • Retail head office plus stores
// The difference

What changes for your business

Critical sites get leased-line reliability, smaller sites get affordable broadband, and everything runs as one secure network. Costs drop compared with leased lines or MPLS everywhere.

Questions about ILL + SD-WAN Hybrid Bundle

Is this cheaper than MPLS everywhere?

+

Usually, yes, especially with many small sites. We'll compare costs for your network.

Can we keep MPLS at some sites?

+

Yes. SD-WAN can include existing MPLS links in the design.

Can we migrate site by site?

+

Yes. Phased migration avoids disruption and lets you prove the design early.

Who handles faults on the links?

+

We do. One support desk covers the links and the SD-WAN.

Is ILL + SD-WAN Hybrid Bundle available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
