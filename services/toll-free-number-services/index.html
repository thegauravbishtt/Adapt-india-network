What is Toll Free Number Services?

With a toll-free 1800 number, callers don't pay for the call; your business does. It makes customers more likely to call, and it signals that you're an established company.

Calls can be routed to your office lines, contact centre or mobiles, with a menu, business-hours rules and reports on every call.

01

Free for callers

Customers call from any phone without being charged.

02

Routing and IVR

Direct callers to sales, support or the nearest branch.

03

Call reports

See call counts, peak times and missed calls.

04

Easy to remember

One national number for all your marketing.

Questions about Toll Free Number Services

How are toll-free calls charged?

+

The business pays for incoming calls, usually per minute. We'll share the plan options for your expected volumes.

What documents are needed?

+

Standard business KYC documents. We'll give you the exact list when you apply.

Can we choose a memorable number?

+

Number availability varies. We'll share the options available when you apply.

Can a toll-free number ring on mobiles?

+

Yes. Calls can be forwarded to mobile phones or answered through a cloud contact centre app.

Is Toll Free Number Services available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
