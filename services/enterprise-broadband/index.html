Business broadband or leased line: how to decide

Business broadband is the right choice for many small sites. These questions help you decide when it's enough and when to move to a leased line.

  1. 01

    How many people use it

    A small office or shop with a few users and cloud apps usually does well on broadband. Larger teams on video calls all day benefit from a leased line.

  2. 02

    How much you upload

    Broadband upload is usually lower than download. If you send CCTV footage, backups or large files, upload speed matters.

  3. 03

    What downtime costs you

    If an outage stops billing or sales, add a backup line or move to a leased line with an uptime SLA.

  4. 04

    Static IP needs

    One static IP is often available on broadband. Several static IPs usually mean a leased line.

  5. 05

    Wi-Fi inside the office

    Slow internet is often a Wi-Fi problem, not the line. Plan access points for the number of people and the layout.
