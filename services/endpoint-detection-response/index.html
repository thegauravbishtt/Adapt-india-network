Why businesses choose Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)

Traditional antivirus looks for known malware. EDR watches what's happening on each laptop and server, such as unusual processes or file encryption, and can stop an attack in progress, including ones never seen before.

When something is detected, an infected device can be isolated from the network with one click while the issue is investigated.

What's included

  • Behaviour detectionCatches suspicious activity, not just known files.
  • Device isolationCut an infected device off from the network instantly.
  • Investigation toolsSee how an attack started and what it touched.
  • Ransomware protectionDetects and stops mass file encryption.

Best suited to

  • Companies worried about ransomware
  • Firms with remote laptops
  • Regulated businesses
  • IT teams needing visibility
// The difference

What changes for your business

Suspicious activity on a laptop or server is caught early and can be contained before it spreads, which is the difference between one infected machine and a company-wide ransomware incident.

Questions about Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)

Does EDR replace antivirus?

+

Most EDR products include antivirus features, so they usually replace it.

Who responds to alerts?

+

Your IT team, or our managed monitoring service if you prefer us to watch alerts.

Does EDR slow down laptops?

+

Modern EDR agents are lightweight, and most users don't notice them.

Does it cover servers too?

+

Yes. Both workstations and servers can be protected.

Is Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
