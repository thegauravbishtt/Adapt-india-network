// FAQs

Smartflo cloud telephony questions

What is Smartflo cloud telephony?+

Smartflo is a cloud phone system for businesses. It covers toll-free and virtual numbers, multi-level IVR, call routing, click-to-call, call recording and CRM integration, managed from a web dashboard and mobile app. Adapt India Networks sets it up and supports it for businesses across India.

Do my employees need desk phones?+

No. Agents can make and take calls on the Smartflo mobile app or from the web panel. Smartflo also works with existing landlines and desk phones if you want to keep them.

Can calls be recorded?+

Yes. Call recording and detailed call logs are part of Smartflo and can be played back from the dashboard. How long recordings are kept depends on the plan you choose.

Can Smartflo connect to our CRM?+

Yes. Smartflo offers API and webhook integration and plug-and-play integration with major CRMs, so caller details and call logs can show up in the tools your team already uses.

Can we get a toll-free number with IVR?+

Yes. Smartflo inbound solutions include toll-free numbers with multi-level IVR, virtual numbers and extension-based routing, so each caller reaches the right team.

How long does it take to go live?+

It depends on how many numbers and users you need, the number paperwork and how complex your call flows are. We confirm the timeline once we understand your requirements.
