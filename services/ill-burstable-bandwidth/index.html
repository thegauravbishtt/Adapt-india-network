How ILL Burstable Bandwidth works

Most businesses don't use the same amount of bandwidth every day. Month-end processing, large uploads or sale days push usage up, while normal days need much less.

With burstable bandwidth you commit to a base speed and can go above it when needed, up to an agreed ceiling. You pay for the base plus what you actually burst, instead of paying for the peak all year.

Committed base speed

A guaranteed level of bandwidth for everyday work.

Headroom for peaks

Use more when you need it, up to the burst limit.

Usage-based billing

Extra usage is billed on agreed terms.

Same leased line benefits

Dedicated fibre, static IPs and SLA as standard ILL.

Popular with

  • E-commerce with sale-day spikes
  • Firms with month-end or quarter-end peaks
  • Media teams with occasional large uploads
  • Growing companies unsure of future needs

What changes for your business

You stop paying all year for a peak you only hit a few days a month, and busy days no longer slow the office down. Finance gets a lower fixed cost; IT gets headroom without a new order every time usage spikes.

Questions about ILL Burstable Bandwidth

How is burst usage measured?

+

Usually with the 95th percentile method, which ignores short spikes. We'll explain the exact terms in your proposal.

Can we change the base speed later?

+

Yes. If your normal usage grows, the committed base can be upgraded.

What happens if we hit the burst ceiling?

+

Traffic is limited at the ceiling. If you reach it often, it's a sign to raise the base or the ceiling.

Is burstable more expensive per Mbps?

+

Burst usage is often priced higher than the base rate, which is why the base should cover your normal needs.

Is ILL Burstable Bandwidth available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
