How BGP Routing Optimization works

Companies with their own IP addresses and more than one internet provider use BGP to decide how traffic enters and leaves their network. Poorly tuned BGP can mean slow failover or one link overloaded while another sits idle.

We review and tune your BGP setup so traffic is balanced sensibly and failover between providers happens quickly.

Multi-ISP design

Use two or more providers effectively.

Traffic balancing

Spread inbound and outbound traffic across links.

Faster failover

Tune timers so outages are routed around quickly.

Route monitoring

Watch for routing problems and hijacks.

Questions about BGP Routing Optimization

Do we need our own IP addresses for BGP?

+

Typically yes, along with an AS number. We can guide you through obtaining them.

Can you work with our existing ISPs?

+

Yes. We coordinate with your current providers.

Can BGP balance traffic evenly?

+

Outbound traffic is easier to control than inbound. Techniques like path prepending and communities help shape inbound traffic.

What is route hijack monitoring?

+

It alerts you if someone else on the internet starts announcing your IP addresses, which can divert your traffic.

Is BGP Routing Optimization available outside Delhi NCR?

+

Yes. We're based in New Delhi and serve businesses across India, including Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Lucknow, through our carrier and technology partners. Availability is confirmed for each location before you sign.
