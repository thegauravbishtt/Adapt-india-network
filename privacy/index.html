PRIVACY POLICY

How Adapt India Networks safeguards enterprise telecom data, telecommunication telemetry, customer records, and network logs under strict statutory confidentiality standards.

Effective: October 2026•ISO 27001 & TRAI Compliance
SCROLL DOWN↓

At Adapt India Networks (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website (adapt-india.net) and our enterprise telecommunications services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when filling out our contact forms, requesting proposals, or communicating with our team:

  • Contact Details: Name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project Details: Information about your project requirements, preferred services, and estimated budgets.
  • Communications: Messages, feedback, and notes from inquiries submitted through our website or email.

We also automatically collect standard technical data when you visit our website, such as your browser type, operating system, IP address, and pages viewed, to ensure our site operates reliably.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for business purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and evaluate project briefs.
  • To prepare custom proposals, timelines, and Statements of Work.
  • To deliver agreed design, development, and marketing services.
  • To maintain website performance, security, and prevent spam.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers (such as hosting platforms and email delivery services) that assist us in operating our website and delivering client services. All such providers are bound by confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption (HTTPS) and restricted access controls, to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory legal requirements.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or ask for your information to be deleted.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@adapt-india.net.

6. Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies to enable core functionality and analyze aggregate website traffic. You can adjust your cookie settings at any time on our Cookie Preferences page.
